# Sales Navigator + Salesforce Outreach Workflow

## Purpose

Use LinkedIn Sales Navigator to identify and message prospects, while Salesforce tracks lead records and outreach activity.

## Daily Steps

1. Save target leads in LinkedIn Sales Navigator.
2. Confirm the lead appears in Salesforce.
3. Open the Salesforce report: **Daily Sales Nav Outreach Queue**.
4. Ask Codex/Troy to run the daily outreach queue.
5. Review the recommended leads and message drafts.
6. Send approved messages manually in Sales Navigator.
7. Sales Navigator syncs completed activity back to Salesforce.
8. Review activity in the **Sales Outreach Activity** dashboard.

## Messaging Rules

- First message should start a conversation, not pitch.
- Do not ask for a meeting in the first message.
- Do not mention Halosight unless it feels natural.
- Do not include links.
- Keep the tone peer-to-peer, thoughtful, concise, and human.
- Target length: 75-140 words.
- If Codex marks a lead as **Manual Profile Review Required**, review the Sales Navigator profile before using a message.

## What Salesforce Tracks

- New Sales Navigator leads
- LinkedIn connection/profile/message steps
- Email attempts
- Calls
- Outreach activity by date, person, company, and owner

## Key Salesforce Views

- **Daily Sales Nav Outreach Queue:** Leads to review for outreach
- **Sales Navigator Activity:** LinkedIn-only activity report
- **Sales Outreach Activity:** LinkedIn, email, and call activity report
- **Sales Outreach Activity Dashboard:** Team activity summary

## Confidence Gate

Codex only puts High- and Medium-confidence messages into the ready-for-review queue. Low-confidence leads are held for manual review so we do not send generic or weakly personalized outreach.

## Important Note

Sales Navigator messages are still sent manually. The workflow helps prioritize leads, draft better messages, and track activity automatically after it syncs back to Salesforce.
